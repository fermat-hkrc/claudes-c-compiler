medium b1

问题概述

constant_fold sign-extends sub-int operands before logical ops (LShr/UDiv/URem at I8/I16 fold wrong)

预期行为LShr((I8)0xB8, 1) folds to 92 (logical shift of the 8-bit pattern)
实际行为folds to -36 — the is_32bit fast path's `as u32` sign-extends 0xB8 to 0xFFFFFFB8 before the logical op
影响评估latent silent miscompile: I8/I16-typed logical shifts / unsigned div-rem of negative constants fold to wrong constants
最小反例I8 DAG LShr(Const(I8(-72)), Const(I8(1)), I8): fold gives -36, C11 requires 92
